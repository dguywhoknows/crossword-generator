Ask it to do things

Press Ctrl+K in the app (Cmd+K on a Mac) and describe what you want. The model chooses from Crossword AI's own functions, runs them in order and tells you what changed. Some examples:

A crossword about 90s video games, tricky cluesWrites the words and builds the grid.
Turn my vocabulary list into a word searchMakes a word search from the Build page words.
Give me a hint for 3 downReveals one letter of that answer.

Instructions you save in the same box, such as "keep it short" or "I'm a beginner", are added to every AI request the app makes.

Features

AI-written themed answers
Clues in three styles (easy, NYT-Tuesday, punny/tricky).
Grid constructor
Multi-attempt search, crossing validation, adjacency rules, area/squareness scoring.
Standard numbering
Across/down clue lists with completion strike-through.
Full keyboard solving
Arrows, Tab/Shift+Tab, Space to flip direction, smart Backspace.
Check
Reveal word, reveal all, clear, solve timer with completion detection.
New layout
From the same words; print-friendly stylesheet (blank grid + clues).
Build page
Paste your own word list (ANSWER: clue, answer - clue, or bare words) with validation, a live layout preview, and model-written clues for any words missing one.
Word search page
The same words hidden horizontally, vertically, diagonally and optionally backwards; drag across letters to mark them; printable.
Share links encode the title
Layout seed and entries, so a friend gets the identical grid with nothing stored on a server.
Export to .ipuz
The open crossword format.

Runs in your browser

Grid construction, numbering, solving UI, checking, timing, printing. None of this needs a network connection or a model.

Uses a language model for

Themed answer/clue generation with style control, sanitized locally.

Notes

Cost
Free. Each account has a daily allowance of AI requests.
Your data
What you make stays in this browser on this device. Your account is only used for signing in and for the built-in AI. You can export a backup or clear everything from Settings.
AI access
Signed-in users get the built-in AI with nothing to set up. You can also use your own Groq or OpenRouter key under Settings.
